# Gaps worth filling

GENERATED by `tools/render.pl` on 2026-09-05 - do not edit; regenerate.

A gap here is not a claim that a tool cannot do something. It means nobody has
said. That is the honest state of a cross-reference nobody has finished, and
it is also the shortest path to being useful to it.

Two ways in, and the first needs no cracker installed:

* **Open an issue** with what you know - an identifier, or better, a test
  vector. "A missing algorithm" and "a mapping is wrong" are the two forms.
* **Open a pull request** against the one small YAML file under
  `data/algorithms/`. `CONTRIBUTING.md` walks through it.

Each row below links to its entry in the [browsable table](index.html).
Gaps are absences; for the rows where something is known to be *odd*, see
[OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).
